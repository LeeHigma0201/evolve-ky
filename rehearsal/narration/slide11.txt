Then the why, pointing at the chain: "double the miles spread over a night's sleep is still only about eleven amps — less than the smallest setting a charger even offers. It can't move." [[slnc 600]] Now drop hours from 8 to 4 — now it moves. "Same driver. Same car. Same miles. The only thing that changed is how long it's plugged in." [[slnc 600]] Switch the car to the truck — watch it climb. [[slnc 600]] THE LINE: "Hours are the lever. Not the badge on the box." [[slnc 600]] HONESTY BEAT: "The breaker line is code and it's exact.
